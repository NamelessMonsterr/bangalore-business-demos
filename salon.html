Before you visit

A few good things to know.

Do I need to know exactly what I want?

Not at all. A consultation is a good place to discuss references and your routine.

Why are prices listed as “from”?

Colour and styling depend on length, product and time. A real salon should agree the final price before the service.

Does the preview book a slot?

No. This demo does not check availability, collect contact details or send appointments.
